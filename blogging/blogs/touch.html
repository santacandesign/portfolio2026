I want to touch things with my hands

Nov 21, 2026

personal

This is a record of my OCD symptoms currently. Trigger warning for OCD, body horror and sexual assault

I have contamination OCD. Specifically the type where I'm scared of touching things because I will get germ'd and I have to wash my hands. And I don't like washing my hands. And it is affecting me in ways people cannot imagine. You don't realise how many things you have to touch to stay alive safely. You constantly need to interact with objects around you and yourself to exist. Here's a laundry list

  • meds
  • pillows and bedsheets
  • yourself ( especially in your private parts)
  • books
  • couches
  • clothes
  • dust in your house to clean
  • dishes
  • pencil
  • laptop
  • soap
  • foot to the floor
  • air when you're outside

  • partner so on and so forth, imagine now that everything that is touching you, you are constantly aware of it-

  • feeling the clothes on my skin

  • extra dryness on my feet
  • extra moisture in my armpit and pubes
  • the goo in your nose
  • the hand that touched that goo earlier to remove said goo
  • part of your hand that touched the slightly dusty part earlier
  • the part of my lower thigh that touched my chair

it is distressing to be aware of these things and furthermore distressing to touch other things whilst already these objects I've touched have left an imprint/dirt on me.

And every person, object and even air is another medium that can imprint on me to further make me aware of the imprints.

And the only thing that makes it better is getting a bath, an extremely hot bath and thats the only way I can get rid of everything. Sadly, immediately after a bath I have to moisturize to not get itchy + dry, and it is extremely overwhelming to get wet, even just applying a moisturizer makes me aware of its existence on my skin and I don't like it.

And two things make it much worse than others - wetness and sexual assault other things that make it worse - lack of sleep, stress and an environment I can't control

wetness is everywhere and sexual assault - I'm constantly hypervigilant of, because the torment lasts for months when it does happen. The imprint is on me and painfully reminded all the time. I spiral more on thoughts that are not even related sexually but I just lay there in my bed thoughts repeating, over and over and over again. Things like all the chores that are piling up, an interaction I had with someone where I did not emote properly. Over and over and over again. Hours of my day, months on end. No end in sight. My body isn't my own, its imprinted, dirty and therefore not mine, I want to detach, I want severe my imprinted body parts so I don't have to think of it anymore.

This has made me horribly agoraphobic. I do not want to leave my house. But I do, as much as I can. I have to live. There is a time period between having faced the fear and leaving my house to reaching somewhere, there is a time period where all that exists is me and the fast moving air in an auto, making me light enough to not be a being that is being imprinted on and is a victim of their environment. I'm floating, the fast air cleansing of my dirt I accumulate.

I am in therapy, taking medication and doing exposure therapy. I refuse to feel dirty just because I'm alive. Because being alive is all there is. I cannot spend it feeling like my body isn't my own and wanting to sever from it.
