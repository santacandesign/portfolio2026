Access to money

Nov 10, 2026

personal

"Didn't buy any clothes for habba?" my dad "Nope, didn't have any money" me "to buy clothes?" "yep" "ok"

conversation today when I went to visit my parents.

I got my first paycheck this whole year in October. I haven't made any money at all to the point where I had to tell my HR not to bother paying taxes because I haven't gotten paid the whole year.

I noticed a few things about myself whilst I had no money to spend this year and a few more things while I now suddenly have disposable income.

When I had no money, every expense, every penny was to be accounted for so things like buying a sweet cold drink, a chocolate or things nobody would notice was out of reach. I would think a lot and most likely didn't buy. Mismatched loose socks were common and I had many many dreams of my converse shoes ripping at the seams. Clothes that ripped were sewn and restoration/alteration attempts were made on the rest. Many torrents were used and lots of failed self haircuts.

I'm slowly buying things again that were necessary and I realize I have no frame of reference for what is necessary. I can't tell if I'm over-indulging or just indulging, buying food just to eat or to say to myself that I can buy them and as a way to self soothe. I don't remember the last time I bought so many books but I am extremely happy.

I hope I recover from the scarcity and feel confident about my finances soon.
