I just don't feel like talking to anyone after I work and I just want to not exist post work, I am so sad and down on energy. It feels truly extractive out of me. I'm so sad man. Especially design needs so much iterating and just creative exhaustion to get to a happy place. It is a draining job. I hope it gets easier one day because right now I'm dying.
