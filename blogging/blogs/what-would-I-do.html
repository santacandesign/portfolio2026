What would I do?

Nov 2, 2026

personal

Hello Happy november, I am tired today and existing. I'm happy to exist, even though tired and I'm thankful to be alive. There is winds, there's parrots and there's a tree. I wish to draw the tree. I came across this beautiful page of trinkets the other day God, it brought me so much joy. I want to draw and add details to everything when I see these things. It made me wonder, if I lived in a village like her's, what would I do to make money. I'd offer to fix appliances. Make little gadgets to track time, water purity and other small inventions. I would make crafts - blankets with lore, trinkets that last a lifetime and grow apricots & custard apples. I would go to people's homes and drink endless cups of tea whilst talking about everything under the sun I would fix my house, paint all over the walls, stack them with books, attach gadgets to test so I can refine and sell I would make clothes for myself and the cats, and feed the fish There would be music and soft light There would also be some dipping in the sea and very very long walks maybe I'd learn to finally ride a bicycle I would make posters for townhall meetings, paint the signboards, go to townhall meetings and offer advice and watch them do something else and be annoyed.

I'm thankful for what I have but I think there could be something else that doesn't hurt me as much, there could be more, I wish I can draw, paint, do magic with electronics and play wonderful calm sounds.

I hope to invite you to such a village sometime.

Thank you for stopping by my brain today
