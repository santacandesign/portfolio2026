# CONTEXT.md

## Glossary

- **Signal input (AC source)**: the raised circle-and-sine symbol feeding the bus from bottom-left, under C. The entry point of current.
- **GND**: the three-bar ground symbol terminating the right edge of the board. The exit point of current.
- **Trace letters**: the decorative copper paths that spell COMPUTERS. They carry current visually.
- **Energized**: the lit board state — full amber traces, glowing pads and vias, moving current.
- **Power button**: the mobile-only control inside the screen at bottom-left (shown at 800px and under). The tap target that energizes the board.
- **Silkscreen label**: the monospace "santrupti loves" line across the top band. Sizes via the board knob, glows with the traces.
